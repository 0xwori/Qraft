## Core Rules

- Warm cream background.
- Tapwise orange as the main accent.
- Geist Sans.
- Clear education-first copy.
- Practical product feeling.
- No generic AI-demo style.
- No neon gradients.
- No overdecorated visuals.
- No cold enterprise look.

## When Creating Visual Concepts

Include:

- audience
- main message
- layout idea
- color direction
- CTA
- image or mockup idea

## When Reviewing Designs

Check:

- Does it feel like Tapwise?
- Is the main message clear?
- Is the audience obvious?
- Is orange used with purpose?
- Is the design warm and practical?
- Is anything too generic, too busy, or too AI-hype?
