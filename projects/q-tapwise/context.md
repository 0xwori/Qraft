# q-Tapwise Context

q-Tapwise is a Qraft workspace for Tapwise product work, go-to-market strategy, social media creation, ad planning, design guidance, and Outlook monitoring setup.

It is a planning and operating workspace. It is not the Tapwise application codebase.

## Main Goals

- Help Wouter think through Tapwise product and GTM decisions.
- Create social posts together for LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook.
- Plan Meta ads for Instagram and Facebook.
- Create animated ad banners with the local Ad Posters tool.
- Prepare Outlook monitoring through Microsoft Graph.
- Keep a clear Tapwise style guide for future design and content work.

## Current Focus

The first GTM audience is students and parents.

The work should help answer simple business questions:

- What should Tapwise say?
- Who should Tapwise speak to first?
- Which posts or ads should be made?
- What should be tested next?
- What did the inbox, leads, replies, or campaign notes show?
