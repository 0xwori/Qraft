## Product Focus

Tapwise helps people turn study material into useful learning support, such as practice, summaries, quizzes, and structured study help.

## First GTM Audience

The first go-to-market focus is students and parents.

Useful framing:

- students want an easier way to start studying
- parents want to help without building the whole study plan themselves
- Tapwise turns existing material into practice and support
