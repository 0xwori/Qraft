## Narrative & Decisions
Five-slide Bitcoin explainer for a general audience:

1. Define Bitcoin as a digital scarcity and settlement network.
2. Explain the transaction-to-block mechanism.
3. Highlight stable monetary design facts: 21 million supply cap, approximate 10-minute blocks, 210,000-block halving schedule.
4. Frame strengths and trade-offs without making price or investment claims.
5. Close with Bitcoin as infrastructure rather than just market speculation.
